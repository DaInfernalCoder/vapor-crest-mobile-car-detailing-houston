# Vapor Crest design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want haze, residue, and uneven presentation removed without inflated restoration claims. The page's single job is to move a driver toward calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Crest navy — `#16243A`
- Vapor violet — `#7067CF`
- Mist — `#DDE4F2`
- Porcelain — `#F8FAFC`
- Signal coral — `#F08A6B`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

The editorial frame treats the hero curve as a band of vapor lifting away from a sharp service crest.

## Signature

One mist line rises over a V-shaped crest and returns as the page's inspection path.

## Critique

The first idea leaned on generic fog gradients. The revision uses flat mist bands and one coral crest, keeping the automotive hierarchy crisp.
